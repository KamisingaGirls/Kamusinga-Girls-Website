Welcome

Step by step

How girls join

Grade 10 places are allocated through the Ministry of Education's placement system. Once your daughter has a place with us, these are the steps to follow.

  1. 1

    Keep the placement letter safe

    Your daughter's placement or admission letter shows that she has a place at Moi Girls Kamusinga. You will need it on reporting day.

  2. 2

    Get the joining instructions

    They list the reporting date, the uniform and the personal items to bring. Ask the school office for the current copy.

    Request them by email

  3. 3

    Pay fees to the official account

    Use only the bank account or Paybill printed on the school's fee structure, and quote your daughter's admission number as the reference. See how to pay safely.

  4. 4

    Report on the given date

    Bring the placement letter, your fee receipts and the items on the joining list.

Transfers

Transfers depend on the space available in each class. Please write to the Principal's office or call 0737 373 679.

Fees

Fees & how to pay safely

The school's fee structure lists the exact amount payable for each term and the official payment details. Ask the office for the current copy.

Request the fee structure

Before you pay

  • All fees is ONLY ACCEPTED in the form of Bankers Cheques and Cash Deposit to school A/C NO.:1107228093 - KCB Kimilili Branch.
  • Or LIPA KARO NA KCB via MPESA PAYBILL NO.:522123 A/C NO.: 58095K followed by student's admission number.
  • CASH WILL NOT be accepted.
  • Original Bank Slip to be brought to school for receipting.
  • In case of EFT transactions/direct credit to the school account, ensure a printout is brought to school for receipting. Parents will meet the EFT charges on deposits.
  • If anyone asks you to send school fees to a personal phone number, DO NOT PAY. Call the school on 0737 373 679 first.

Reporting day

What to have ready

The joining instructions have the full list. These are the essentials families ask about most.

  • Placement or admission letter
  • Fee payment receipts
  • Copy of the birth certificate
  • Results slip and leaving certificate from the previous school
  • Uniform, matress and bedcover are found in school
  • Bedding, toiletries and personal items on the list

Questions

Questions families ask

Is it a day school or a boarding school?

Moi Girls Kamusinga is a girls' boarding school.

Which senior school pathways are offered?

All three CBC pathways: STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts & Sports Science. Read about the pathways.

Who sponsors the school?

The Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

Is there medical care on campus?

Yes. The sanatorium provides on-site treatment and nursing care 24 hours a day.

When can parents visit?

Parents' Day and other visiting dates are on the school calendar.

How do I reach the school?

Call the school office on 0737 373 679 or email moigirlskamusinga@gmail.com.

Still have a question?

The school office can help with placement, fees and reporting.
