Giving Back: The Alumni Water Tank Initiative

One of the network's standout recent projects was fully funding and installing a modern high-capacity water tank for the school — a practical gift that keeps clean water flowing for hundreds of boarders every single day.

It embodies the spirit of our association: zero idle talk, direct action, and tangible improvements that enhance daily student life.
