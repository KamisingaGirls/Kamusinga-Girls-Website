HOW TO ADD YOUR SCHOOL PHOTOS
==============================

Most photos on the site are already your real ones. The files below still show
a blue 'PHOTO NEEDED' card and need a real photo:

  - gallery-1.jpg   (Home page, gallery strip - library or reading corner)
  - gallery-3.jpg   (Home page, gallery strip - evening prep or quiet study)
  - gallery-4.jpg   (Home page, gallery strip - campus grounds, chapel or assembly)

To replace one: rename your photo to that exact file name (e.g. gallery-1.jpg)
and drop it into this folder. PNG works too - just delete the .jpg and add
gallery-1.png instead; the site finds it automatically.

A few other placeholder files are kept here unused, in case you want them later:
alumni-hero.jpg, alumni-watertank.jpg, academics-support.jpg, academics-cbc.jpg,
home-about.jpg. None of these are currently referenced by any page.

Tips
- Landscape (wide) photos work best; every photo is cropped to fit its frame.
- Keep each file under about 400 KB (try squoosh.app or tinypng.com) so pages
  load quickly on mobile data.
