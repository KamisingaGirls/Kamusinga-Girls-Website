Our Heritage

A school built from one classroom and 27 girls

Founded in 1973 under the auspices of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK), ACK Moi Girls High School Kamusinga has grown from a humble single-classroom community project into one of Kenya’s leading national public girls boarding institutions.

Located in Kimilili, Bungoma County, the school occupies an expansive, well-equipped 30-acre campus — recognised as one of the leading girls' boarding schools in Bungoma County and across western Kenya — dedicated to empowering female scholars through quality instruction, disciplined character development, and competitive co-curricular activities.

Core Values

What matters to us

Our core values define our culture, shape our daily interactions, and guide every student toward becoming an ethical leader in society.

Godliness

Nurturing faith, Christian morality, and spiritual resilience as the foundation of personal conduct and leadership.

Integrity

Upholding honesty, transparency, and high moral standards in academic work and personal character.

Teamwork

Collaborating harmoniously across staff, students, parents, and alumni to achieve collective educational goals.

Professionalism

Maintaining dedication, high standards of teaching, order, and prompt service delivery in all operations.

Competence & Excellence

Striving for the highest levels of achievement in KCSE exams, sports, robotics, drama, and intellectual pursuits.

Confidentiality

Ensuring respectful guidance, student counseling privacy, and professional administrative stewardship.

Beyond The Classroom

Nurturing talent, leadership, & character.

At ACK Moi Girls High School Kamusinga, learning goes far beyond books. We give every girl room to discover her talents, stay active, and grow into a well-rounded leader through sports, creative arts, and student-led clubs.

Drama & Music

Award-winning performances in music, traditional folk songs, dance and public poetry that build deep self-confidence and creative expression.

Sports & Games

Handball is our flagship sport - the school won bronze at the East Africa regional level. Girls also play soccer, basketball, volleyball, athletics, badminton, and lawn tennis, plus indoor chess and table tennis.

Clubs & Societies

Active student groups including St. John Ambulance, President's Award Scheme, Christian Union, Young Catholic Students (YCS), Debate Club, Journalism Club, and the Kenya Science & Engineering Fair.
